## Slide 1 — Title
**HealingSathi** · *No one heals alone.*
The moderated support network for people living with chronic conditions — community and verified professionals in one place.
Launching: India · US · Canada

> Note: "Sathi" means *companion* in Hindi. Healthcare has doctors and pills. It doesn't have companions. We're building that layer.

## Slide 2 — The problem
**Chronic illness is a loneliness machine.**
- 6 in 10 US adults live with a chronic disease (CDC) — and manage it alone between appointments.
- India carries an estimated 100M+ people with diabetes alone (est., ICMR-region studies).
- Diagnosed people face 2–3× higher rates of anxiety and depression (est.) — the medical system treats the organ, nobody treats the isolation.

> Note: the 2am moment — newly diagnosed, terrified, googling. That's our user, at their moment of maximum need.

## Slide 3 — Today's options fail them
| Where they go | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Facebook groups | Unmoderated, zero privacy, algorithm noise |
| Reddit | Anonymous roulette; no continuity, no professionals |
| Telehealth apps | Transactional — you're alone the other 27 days of the month |
| PatientsLikeMe-era forums | Data-first, desktop-era, relationship-last |

> Note: the gap isn't information. It's *belonging with safety*.

## Slide 4 — The product (built, working end-to-end)
- **Condition-matched feed** — your community from minute one
- **Moderated groups** — every group and consultant passes human review (built: superuser review queue)
- **Sathis** — 1:1 companions with chat, photo sharing, threaded conversations
- **Audio/video calls** — peer-to-peer WebRTC, no third-party service
- **Verified consultants** — psychologists & specialists, application-reviewed, bookable in-app

> Note: this is not a deck-stage product. 110+ automated end-to-end checks pass; demo available live.

## Slide 5 — Why now
1. Post-COVID normalization of digital health & online support
2. Loneliness declared a public-health priority (US Surgeon General advisory, 2023)
3. India: cheap data + smartphone-first health behavior already lives in WhatsApp groups — unowned, unmoderated, ready to graduate
4. Trust collapse in ad-driven social platforms → demand for purpose-built, private spaces

## Slide 6 — Market
- **TAM:** chronic-condition adults in launch markets — hundreds of millions (est.)
- **SAM:** English/Hindi-speaking, smartphone-active chronic patients in IN/US/CA (est. 150–250M)
- **SOM (24 mo):** 9 beachhead condition-communities → 150k users (plan §7 of marketing.md)
- Monetization comparables: telehealth take-rates, community subscriptions (Patreon-era willingness to pay for belonging)

## Slide 7 — Business model
1. **Consultant marketplace take-rate** (booking fee) — live in product
2. **HealingSathi Plus** (later): premium groups, priority consultations
3. **B2B (later):** advocacy orgs, employers, insurers — engagement tooling for their members
Community access stays free, forever. Trust is the asset; we never sell data or feed ads.

## Slide 8 — Go-to-market
**Beachhead discipline: 3 conditions × 3 markets.** Community-led, near-zero paid spend through launch.
- India: WhatsApp-group graduation + 20 founding doctors
- US/CA: earned subreddit/advocacy presence + patient micro-creators
- The 24-hour rule: every first post gets a real reply < 24h
- Product-native loops: sathi invites, share cards, group proposals
(Full plan: `marketing.md`)

## Slide 9 — The flywheel
New user → condition-matched feed shows a living community → first reply < 24h → joins groups, adds sathis → invites the one person they know who *gets it* → books a consultant on hard weeks → revenue funds moderation → trust brings the next user.

## Slide 10 — Competition
2×2: **moderated ↔ unmoderated** × **community-first ↔ clinical-first**.
We sit alone in the moderated + community-first quadrant with professionals attached. FB/Reddit = unmoderated community. BetterHelp/Practo = clinical, no community. PatientsLikeMe = data platform, not a companion.

## Slide 11 — Moat
1. **Trust infrastructure** — human review of every group & consultant (operational muscle, not a feature toggle)
2. **Community network effects** — your sathis and your group's history don't port to a competitor
3. **Condition-language depth** — Hindi + regional-language support communities nobody in the West will build
4. **The condition graph** — anonymized, consented insight into what actually helps patients day-to-day (long-term data asset, never sold raw)
