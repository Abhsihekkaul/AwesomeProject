05 · Why now

Four waves, one moment.

  • Digital health is normalized — post-COVID, online support is default behavior, not fringe.
  • Loneliness is now policy — declared a public-health priority (US Surgeon General advisory, 2023).
  • India is ready — cheap data, smartphone-first health behavior already living in unmoderated WhatsApp groups, waiting to graduate.
  • Trust collapse in ad-driven platforms → demand for purpose-built, private spaces.

07 · Business model

Community free forever. Care pays the bills.

  • Consultant marketplace take-rate — booking fees on in-app sessions (live in product).
  • HealingSathi Plus (later) — premium groups, priority consultations.
  • B2B (later) — advocacy orgs, employers, insurers: engagement tooling for their members.

Trust is the asset. We never sell data and never put ads in the feed — that's the moat, not a constraint.

08 · Go-to-market

3 conditions × 3 markets. Community-led. Near-zero paid spend.

  • India: "graduate" existing WhatsApp condition-groups + 20 founding doctors whose patients become our first users.
  • US/Canada: earned presence in condition subreddits and patient-advocacy orgs; patient micro-creators over mega-influencers.
  • The 24-hour rule: every new user's first post gets a genuine reply within 24 hours. Retention lives here.
  • Product-native loops: sathi invites, share cards, user-proposed groups — all shipped.
